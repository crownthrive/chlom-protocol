# PENTA Doctrine v3

> **PENTA = Discover → Govern → Execute → Verify → Preserve**

PENTA is CrownThrive's five-stage institutional execution doctrine. It is the common operating grammar for the PENTA family and an interoperability contract among PentaRoute, Phase 3, CHLOM, ThriveBase, Penta Federation, PentaDocs, factories, and future participants.

## CHLOM's role in PENTA

CHLOM is principally the **Govern** authority, but participates across all five stages:

1. **Discover** — registers identities, contracts, capabilities, evidence references, dependencies, provider state, and institutional context.
2. **Govern** — resolves rights, rules, roles, credentials, risk class, authority, vault custody, certification requirements, and remedies.
3. **Execute** — authorizes exact operations for PentaRoute/provider-native adapters; CHLOM itself does not imply unrestricted execution.
4. **Verify** — records certification evidence, receipts, DAIL/evidence references, canary/readback results, and policy decisions.
5. **Preserve** — retains versioned authority, historical decisions, continuity records, proofs, audit state, and institutional provenance.

## Naming is not authority

A PENTA component name never manufactures permission. `PentaDelete` does not mean universal delete. `PentaDeploy` does not mean every target is deployable. `PentaAuth` does not bypass provider authentication. Mutation authority remains exact-operation, risk-class, credential, and evidence scoped.

## Future PENTA participants

The PENTA family is open to additional CrownThrive components, licensed participants, acquired systems, external providers, and developer extensions when they express a distinct institutional role. Each new participant must document its five-stage mapping, machine identity, human description, dependencies, authority boundary, autonomous risk ceiling, verification method, preservation method, and federation relationship.

Canonical institutional rule:

> **If CrownThrive cannot discover it, govern it, execute it, verify it, and preserve it, the institutional loop is incomplete.**
