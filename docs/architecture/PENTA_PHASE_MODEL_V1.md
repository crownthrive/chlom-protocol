# CHLOM PENTA Phase Model v1

**Canonical institutional lifecycle:** Discover → Govern → Execute → Verify → Preserve  
**Current CrownThrive generation:** Phase 3 — Execute

CHLOM inherits the CrownThrive institutional phase model and does not maintain a competing timeline.

| Phase | PENTA stage | CHLOM emphasis | Institutional status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Discover | identity, source, asset, provider, rights-signal and evidence discovery | Historical / consolidated |
| 2 | Govern | rights, rules, roles, credentials, policy, licensing, capability ceilings, evidence gates | Historical / consolidated |
| 3 | Execute | apply CHLOM authority to PentaRoute, PentaFabric, PentaFactory, APIs/MCPs, provider adapters and operating workflows | Current |
| 4 | Verify | independent authority conformance, provider readback, certification renewal, adversarial tests, reconciliation and proof | Future |
| 5 | Preserve | rights continuity, archive lineage, durable identity, succession, portability and seven-generation custody | Future |

Historical Phase 2.x decimal labels are preserved as evidence but retired as current institutional roadmap taxonomy. CHLOM may retain exact historical identifiers in migration names, releases, archived documents, schemas, receipts, and compatibility surfaces where changing them would damage provenance.

No phase label grants authority. CHLOM authority continues to derive from current effective policy, identity, credentials, evidence, capability certification, provider constraints, and applicable human-reserved governance.
