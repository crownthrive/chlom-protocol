# CHLOM Help Center Recovery

The surviving Help Center Structure contains **795 recovered article records** after repair of export anomalies. The recovered titles and hierarchy are authoritative evidence that the knowledge areas existed; they are **not** proof that all historical article bodies survived or that every described module was implemented.

## Recovery classes
- `recovered-verbatim` — surviving original body exists.
- `reconstructed-from-sources` — new body written from surviving sources; not represented as the lost original.
- `canonical-replacement` — intentionally new article superseding conflicting historical material.
- `archived-historical` — preserved for provenance; not current instruction.
- `missing-owner-input` — no defensible body can yet be reconstructed.
