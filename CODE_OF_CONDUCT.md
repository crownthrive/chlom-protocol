# CHLOM Community & Contribution Conduct

CHLOM collaboration must protect people, culture, rights, evidence integrity, intellectual property, privacy, security, and the institutional record.

Participants must act professionally; preserve attribution, provenance, correction history, current-vs-historical state, and public/private boundaries; disclose material uncertainty; and follow CrownThrive OS and CHLOM governance.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, exposing restricted information, impersonating CrownThrive or CHLOM authority, submitting stolen/fabricated material, false certification or ownership claims, bypassing review or authority gates, fabricating evidence/provider state, using historical records to knowingly misrepresent current state, or retaliating against good-faith reports.

AI, agents, scripts, and factories are contributors, not independent authority. They may not fabricate sources, rights, approvals, provider writes, payments, certifications, or consensus; self-create reserved authority; silently rewrite history; or self-approve consequential actions where separation of duties applies.

Public access does not waive CrownThrive rights or grant permission to clone, commercialize, train models on, bulk-extract, certify, or rebrand protected CHLOM material. See `LICENSE`.

Sensitive concerns should be reported privately under `SECURITY.md` or to **contact@crownthrive.com**.

CrownThrive may moderate, reject, quarantine, restrict, or remove participation that threatens people, systems, rights, evidence, security, or institutional continuity. Good-faith disagreement with a technical or governance decision is not itself misconduct.
