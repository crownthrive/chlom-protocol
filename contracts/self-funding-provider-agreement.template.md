# CrownThrive Self-Funding Provider Agreement

**Template only. Not legal advice. Not an executed agreement.**

## 1. Parties

CrownThrive, LLC ("CrownThrive") and the approved provider identified in the applicable order, schedule, or signature block ("Provider").

## 2. Covered Offering

This Agreement applies only to the products, services, events, courses, bookings, media, or other offerings expressly listed as covered.

## 3. Economic Schedule

For a qualified transaction governed by the active economic schedule, the target allocation is:

- 80% Provider
- 10% HQ Growth & Technology
- 5% Regional Leadership, when applicable
- 3% National Advertising Fund
- 2% ThriveFund

The active signed economic schedule controls. Percentages are not effective merely because they appear in software or documentation.

## 4. Processing and Settlement

The parties will identify the payment processor, settlement method, timing, applicable reserves, fees, taxes, refunds, chargebacks, disputes, reversals, and payout conditions in the applicable schedule.

## 5. Rights and Eligibility

Provider represents that it owns or controls the rights necessary to supply the covered offering and will comply with applicable CrownThrive/CHLOM eligibility requirements.

## 6. Attribution and Data

CrownThrive may maintain transaction, attribution, analytics, compliance, fulfillment, and reconciliation records necessary to operate the covered offering, subject to applicable privacy law and the parties' privacy obligations.

## 7. Refunds, Disputes and Reversals

Refunds and disputes may result in proportional reversal, hold, reserve, or clawback of previously allocated amounts as specified in the applicable schedule.

## 8. Audit and Reconciliation

Each party must reasonably cooperate with transaction reconciliation and provide evidence necessary to resolve material discrepancies.

## 9. Suspension

CrownThrive may suspend settlement or access when required by payment-provider rules, fraud indicators, rights concerns, compliance failures, contractual breach, or material reconciliation variance.

## 10. Independent Relationship and Taxes

The parties' legal relationship and tax responsibilities must be specified by counsel for the actual engagement. Nothing in this template creates employment, partnership, agency, equity, or ownership unless expressly stated in a signed agreement.

## 11. Termination

Termination mechanics, treatment of pending transactions, surviving obligations, and final reconciliation must be stated in the executed agreement.
