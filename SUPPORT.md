# CHLOM Support

CHLOM repository support is for public-safe questions about documentation, schemas, registries, interoperability contracts, contribution workflow, historical/current-state interpretation, and reproducible repository behavior.

## Public issues are appropriate for

- documentation corrections that contain no restricted information;
- reproducible validation/test failures;
- schema or registry inconsistencies;
- broken public-safe references;
- historical/current-state labeling errors;
- interoperability questions that do not require private evidence.

## Contact privately for

Security, privacy, protected rights/evidence, private licensing, enterprise/OEM use, certification requests, restricted integration information, or other non-public matters:

**contact@crownthrive.com**

## Authority boundary

Support responses do not independently grant licenses, provider-write authority, certification, partnership, entitlement, legal approval, D3 authority, or permission beyond the applicable CrownThrive OS/CHLOM record or written agreement.

## Source of truth

CrownThrive OS governs institutional state. CHLOM repository records govern their exact component scope. PentaDocs and websites are downstream projections and may lag without changing canonical state.
