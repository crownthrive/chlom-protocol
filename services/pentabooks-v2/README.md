# PentaBooks v2 - Governed Publishing Factory

PentaBooks v2 extends `ct.pentabooks.v1` without replacing or silently rewriting it. It adds the repeatable production contract required to turn the Virality Music source canopy into complete books, companion products, exact rights candidates, and evidence-bound PentaGreen handoffs.

## Production truth

- System: `ct.pentabooks.v2`
- Existing control runtime: `ct.pentabooks.control.v1`
- Factory layer: deterministic, candidate-only
- Provider write: disabled in this package
- Money movement: disabled
- Rights grant: disabled
- Publication: fail-closed until exact provider readback
- Rotation requirement: at least two complete books and ten individually governed digital products

## Canonical source order

1. `https://virality-music.crownthrive.chatgpt.site`
2. Approved ChatGPT Work production artifacts
3. PentaBooks project sources
4. PentaBooks project chats
5. Outside primary research, explicitly labeled as research

Corrections append and preserve. Prior editions, conflicting claims, holds, and fingerprints remain visible.

## Canon states

`CANON_CONFIRMED`, `SOURCE_CLAIM`, `DISPUTED`, `PROTECTED`, `SEALED`, `OPEN`, `SUPERSEDED`, `CORRECTED`.

## Cultural Imprint Engine

Every work is evaluated across ten evidence-bearing axes: Place, People, Language, Object, Ritual, Sound, Power and Conflict, Care and Value, Boundary and Protection, and Future and Legacy. A high total cannot cure a material rights, privacy, representation, canon, accessibility, or provider-state defect.

## Factory contract

The factory validates:

- exact identity and version;
- source custody and SHA-256;
- two-book / ten-product rotation minimums;
- canon-collision visibility;
- CIE score and hard minimums;
- publication status versus production status;
- rights and accessibility states;
- PentaGreen price and license candidates;
- release preflight and provider-readback requirements.

## Rotation 01 pilot

Rotation 01 packages:

1. `VM-PB-R01-001` - *Who Paid Ashley? The Motive Ledger*
2. `VM-PB-R01-002` - *The Book of Eloise: The Bible Wanted Names*
3. `VM-PB-R01-003` through `VM-PB-R01-010` - eight reader, archive, discussion, journal, evidence, and canon companion products.

The pilot preserves an active sequence collision: both *Who Paid Ashley?* and *The Song That Paid Everybody but Its Writer* currently claim TrapOpera Book Seven. The factory therefore does not silently renumber either work.

## Release boundary

A local file, source row, assigned credit amount, candidate package, or test object is not a live release. Release requires the exact asset/version/hash, rights clearance, accessibility review, mandatory QA, customer/destination entitlement logic, and provider readback. Official EPUBCheck and owned-reader/device QA remain release gates for EPUB products.
