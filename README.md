# CHLOM™ — Phase 3 Governance & Authority Fabric

**CHLOM — Compliance Hybrid Licensing and Ownership Model — is CrownThrive's governed authority architecture for Rights, Rules, Roles, Revenue, Records, and Remedies.**

CHLOM no longer uses the repository's former **“Phase 2 foundation / pre-alpha”** wording as current institutional orientation. That language is historical. CrownThrive is now in **Phase 3 — Execute**, under the canonical PENTA lifecycle:

> **Phase 1 — Discover → Phase 2 — Govern → Phase 3 — Execute → Phase 4 — Verify → Phase 5 — Preserve**

CHLOM remains the governance function that CrownThrive established during the broader Phase 2 generation, but it is now actively exercised inside Phase 3 execution. It authorizes, constrains, records, certifies, reconciles, and preserves the institutional conditions under which autonomous software may act.

## CHLOM's place inside PENTA

PENTA gives CrownThrive a five-stage institutional loop. CHLOM is most visible in **Govern**, but it participates across all five stages.

### Discover

CHLOM identifies the governance facts required before action: asset identity, provenance, ownership evidence, participants, credentials, rights claims, agreements, policy dependencies, provider constraints, evidence gaps, prior decisions, and unresolved conflicts.

Discovery is not permission. A discovered right, endpoint, account, provider capability, or credential cannot be treated as active authority merely because it exists.

### Govern

This is CHLOM's core function. CHLOM resolves who may act, under what conditions, against which asset or system, with which credentials, inside what risk ceiling, and with what required evidence.

Governance includes rights, rules, roles, licensing, delegated authority, separation of duties, credential custody, capability ceilings, approval requirements, policy inheritance, provider certification, revocation, holds, appeals, and remedies.

### Execute

In Phase 3, CHLOM does not merely describe governance. Its contracts are consumed by PentaRoute, PentaTun, PentaFabric, PentaFactory, provider adapters, API/MCP surfaces, queues, schedulers, publishing systems, commerce systems, wallets, and other CrownThrive runtimes.

Execution is bounded by exact authority. The existence of a route, credential, API, factory, or mutation verb never creates a blanket right to use it.

### Verify

CHLOM records and evaluates provider readback, DAIL evidence, signatures, receipts, certification results, canaries, rollback proofs, policy decisions, audit artifacts, and reconciliation state.

This verification function becomes institutionally dominant in **Phase 4 — Verify**, when CrownThrive refactors from “can it run?” toward “can the institution independently prove it ran correctly under the correct authority?”

### Preserve

CHLOM preserves durable rights and governance context: versions, provenance, authority history, corrections, revocations, decisions, disputes, continuity data, archive state, and evidence references.

This becomes especially important in **Phase 5 — Preserve**, where provider portability, succession, long-horizon rights custody, PentaGeneration, and seven-generation continuity become the institution's dominant objective.

## The six canonical CHLOM functions

1. **Rights** — assets, ownership, provenance, versions, licensing, permissions, restrictions, revocation, and stewardship.
2. **Rules** — policies, conditions, eligibility, risk classifications, compliance gates, evidence thresholds, and approval requirements.
3. **Roles** — organizations, people, agents, credentials, delegated authority, duties, quorum, and separation of powers.
4. **Revenue** — governed fees, allocations, royalties, commissions, holds, adjustments, settlement state, and reconciliation.
5. **Records** — DAIL events, evidence references, approvals, versions, agreements, receipts, corrections, and audit history.
6. **Remedies** — disputes, holds, suspensions, revocations, appeals, corrections, recovery, and restoration.

These functions are permanent institutional responsibilities. Their implementation can evolve without changing their meaning.

## Phase model refactor

CrownThrive's earlier architecture used increasingly granular transitional phase labels, especially inside Phase 2. Labels such as Phase 2.5, 2.7, 2.8, 2.9, 2.95, 2.97, 2.98, 2.99, pre-entry convergence, and similar workstream labels helped describe intermediate states while governance and runtime architecture were still being discovered.

Those labels are now retired as **current institution-wide roadmap taxonomy**.

They remain historical evidence when embedded in filenames, branches, migration identifiers, changelogs, release notes, contracts, or archived documentation. They are not rewritten simply to make history cleaner. Instead, they map into the broader historical **Phase 2 — Govern** generation.

CrownThrive will not create new decimal institutional phases. Precision now belongs in:

- OS release versions;
- component/API/schema/contract versions;
- maturity states;
- certification states;
- risk/authority classes;
- waves, sprints, cohorts, or milestones.

The current institutional generation is **Phase 3 — Execute**. CHLOM's role is to ensure execution remains governed rather than merely automated.

## PentaRoute and bounded execution

PentaRoute is the canonical governed routing family. CHLOM supplies the authority context that distinguishes routing from permission.

A read route may be registered without authorizing mutation. A mutation may be certified for one exact operation without authorizing every mutation on the same provider. A delete primitive may exist without universal delete. A factory may generate software without receiving authority to deploy it. A provider may return HTTP 200 without proving institutional success.

This separation is foundational to Phase 3.

## PentaGreen and economic authority

PentaGreen is CrownThrive's commerce/economic activation authority and succeeds ThriveEvergreen as the canonical human-facing identity where the current OS has adopted that rebrand. CHLOM governs the authority conditions under which economic actions may occur.

Economic execution must not infer rights, licensing, entitlement, or settlement merely from provider success. Provider evidence is one input into institutional truth, not a substitute for it.

## Public / private boundary

This repository is public-safe, not a dump of CrownThrive's entire private governance substrate.

It does not expose secrets, raw restricted evidence, private credentials, protected Fingerprint implementation details, proprietary calibration, confidential agreements, protected economic logic, private reasoning, or material whose disclosure would weaken security or rights protection.

Public-safe documentation can prove the existence and shape of a governed system without disclosing the secrets that secure or differentiate that system.

## Current maturity language

Institutional phase and component maturity are separate dimensions.

A CHLOM component may be `CANDIDATE`, `CONTROLLED_TEST`, `ACTIVE`, `PRODUCTION`, `WRITE_VERIFIED`, `HISTORICAL`, `SUPERSEDED`, `RESERVE`, or `RETIRED` while CrownThrive remains institutionally in Phase 3.

Likewise, a v1 or v2 contract can remain the correct active component contract during OS 3.x. The PENTA phase refactor does not force-renumber every schema, API, migration, function, or protocol.

## Authority boundary

CHLOM is explicitly designed to prevent technical capability from being mistaken for institutional authority.

No documentation page, release number, repository merge, AI output, generated artifact, workflow, successful HTTP response, provider feature, API token, deployed runtime, wallet capability, or payment rail independently creates ownership, licensing rights, entitlement, settlement, D3 authority, or universal mutation authority.

## Phase 4 direction — Verify

CHLOM's Phase 4 work will emphasize independent evidence quality and assurance: certification renewal, cross-provider readback, authority conformance, formalized proofs, adversarial tests, economic reconciliation, rights integrity, security assurance, provider-exit drills, rollback evidence, data-quality verification, and resistance to silent policy drift.

The goal is to make governance claims independently checkable rather than self-asserted.

## Phase 5 direction — Preserve

CHLOM's Phase 5 work will emphasize long-horizon rights and governance continuity: succession, provider portability, archive lineage, durable asset identity, authority transfer controls, seven-generation stewardship, recovery packages, executable institutional memory, and preservation of rights/evidence across platform and personnel changes.
